Why nothing instructional sits behind a paywall

Entry steps, alignment cues, contraindications and supported versions belong to everyone who practices, whether or not they can pay. Membership pays for convenience, never for safety information.

Digital products

Three one-time purchases you download and keep. Each is a PDF made to print or to read on a tablet propped beside your mat. They use the same poses, cues and supported versions as the free library, arranged into longer plans so you do not have to build one yourself.

Shade members pay 20% less on each one. You do not need a membership to buy them.

How buying works

  1. Ask for a checkout link through the contact page, choosing "Membership and billing" as the topic and naming the product.
  2. We email you a secure link to our payment processor. Your card details go there, never through this site.
  3. After payment, the download link arrives by email. Keep the files for your own practice.

Not sure a product suits you? The free guided sequences use the same format, so you can try the style before you buy anything.

How this site is funded

You should know who pays for what you read. Here is every way this site earns money, in plain English.

None of these sources gets a say in our cues, our safety notes or which versions of a pose we teach.

Read the Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

  • Display ads. The free library, sequences and guides are paid for by ads from ad networks. They are labeled "Advertisement," kept away from instructions and buttons, and never shown on this page, the contact page, the newsletter page or legal pages. Personalized ads load only with your consent.
  • Shade Membership. Members pay $7 a month or $60 a year to browse without ads and to get downloads and printable cards.
  • Affiliate links. When a guide links to a mat, block, strap or bolster at a retailer, we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. Each of those links is disclosed right where it appears.
  • Digital products. Three downloadable programs, sold once and yours to keep.
  • Sponsored content. Occasional brand partnerships are labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page. Sponsors never review or edit safety guidance.
  • Featured teacher listings. Teachers can pay $19 a month to appear first in their state, always marked as a paid placement.

Billing questions

The short, honest answers. For anything not covered here, see the full FAQ or contact us.

Use the cancellation link in any membership receipt email, or send us a message through the contact page with the topic "Membership and billing." Your membership stays active until the end of the month or year you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again. There is no cancellation fee and no phone call required.

Digital products: if a program or bundle is not right for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we will refund it in full. No explanation is needed.

Membership: we do not prorate a month or year that has already started, but if you were charged after cancelling, or charged twice by mistake, we refund the charge in full.

Sequence PDFs and printable pose cards you have already downloaded are yours to keep for personal practice. Digital products you bought separately are not affected by membership changes at all. We keep your saved practice collections in case you rejoin, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Prices are listed in US dollars. Depending on where you live, sales tax may apply to digital goods and subscriptions. If it does, the payment processor calculates it and shows the full total on the checkout page before you pay, so there are no surprises on your statement.

Checkout runs on our payment processor's secure page, which accepts major credit and debit cards. We never see or store your full card number. If you need to pay another way, write to us and we will tell you what is possible.

No display ads, on any page, while you are signed in. Two things remain because they are part of the content rather than ad space: affiliate links on prop recommendations, which are labeled where they appear, and any sponsored article, which is labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page.
