Our editorial standards

Every article follows the same rules as our pose guides. If you ever find one that does not, we want to hear about it.

  • Written by a teacher. Articles are written by our founder and lead teacher, Rosa Delgado, who completed a 200-hour and a 300-hour yoga teacher training. She is not a doctor or clinician, and nothing here is medical advice.
  • Checked against our pose guides. Before publishing, each article is reviewed against the pose and sequence pages it mentions, so that entry steps, hold times and supported versions match.
  • Mechanics, not promises. We describe what moves, what lengthens and what you may notice. We make no health claims for any pose, sequence or breathing exercise.
  • Every version counts. Chair, wall and prop options are written as complete versions of a pose, never as lesser ones.
  • Dated and maintained. Each article shows its publication date. When we make a substantive change, we update the page and note the new date.

Corrections

Spotted an error, a broken link or an instruction that does not match a pose guide? Tell us through our contact page with the article title and what you noticed. We review every report and correct the page when we got something wrong.

Sponsored content and affiliate links

Sponsored content is always labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page, and sponsors never review or edit our safety guidance. None of the articles listed here is sponsored.
