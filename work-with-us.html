Our standards

Readers follow our guides with their own bodies, so their trust matters more than any single partnership. These rules apply to every advertiser and sponsor, without exception.

Every partnership includes

  • A "Sponsored" label at the top of every sponsored page, and at the top of every sponsored newsletter section.
  • No sponsor review of safety guidance. Sponsors do not see, edit or approve our cues, contraindications or supported versions.
  • Our editorial control. We write or edit everything we publish and keep the final say on wording.
  • Honest comparisons. If a belt, a pillow or a wall works as well as a product, we say so.
  • No rankings. We do not crown a winning product or rank brands against each other.
  • Affiliate links disclosed at the point of the link, as set out in our Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure.

What we decline

  • Ads or sponsors that make medical or health claims, or promise outcomes for any condition.
  • Weight-loss products and programs.
  • Supplements of any kind.
  • Products that claim to purify or flush the body.
  • Fertility products.
  • Body-transformation programs and before-and-after marketing.
  • Anything we consider unsafe for people practicing at home, such as gear that encourages forcing a joint past its comfortable range.

Who reads the site

Our readers are mostly adults in the United States who are new to yoga or returning after a long break, practicing at home on their own schedule. Many are working with bodies that do not match the stock image of a yoga student: tight hamstrings, desk-bound shoulders, older knees, limited floor access.

They usually arrive looking for one specific thing, such as a pose explained or a 10-minute routine, and they stay for plain language and complete chair, wall and prop versions. They value practical, durable gear and straightforward advice over hype.

Partners who fit this audience tend to make simple, well-made props, furnishings for small home practice spaces, or educational resources for beginners.

Audience details are shared on request. Ask through the form below and we will send what we can share.
