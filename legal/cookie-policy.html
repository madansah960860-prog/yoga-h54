What cookies and local storage are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. When you come back, your browser sends the cookie back, so the site can remember something, such as a setting. Cookies set by the site you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by another company's service running on that site, such as an advertising network, are called third-party cookies. Some cookies disappear when you close your browser; others stay for a set time.

Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site keep a small amount of information on your device. Unlike a cookie, it is not sent to our server with every page request. It stays in your browser until you, or your browser, clear it. We use local storage, rather than cookies, to remember your privacy choices.

Pixels, scripts and similar tools can do similar jobs. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of these technologies.

How consent works on this site

On your first visit, a banner at the bottom of the screen explains our cookies and offers three buttons:

  • Accept all allows analytics and advertising cookies.
  • Reject non-essential allows only the essential storage described below.
  • Manage preferences opens a panel where you can switch analytics and advertising on or off separately, then save your choice.

Until you choose, nothing optional loads: no analytics scripts, no advertising scripts and no third-party cookies. Ignoring the banner is not consent. Rejecting takes exactly one click, the same as accepting, and every guide, sequence, timer and pacer works the same whichever you choose.

We store your choice in your browser's local storage, so the banner will not ask again on that browser. Because it lives on your device, your choice applies only to that browser and device. If you clear your browser's site data, use a private window or switch devices, we will ask again. If your browser blocks local storage altogether, we cannot remember your choice, so the banner will reappear on your next visit and nothing optional will load.

You can change your mind at any time, using the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page or the button below. When you switch a category off, its scripts no longer load on the pages you visit afterward. To remove cookies that a partner has already set, delete them in your browser settings, as described in Browser controls.

The categories we use

Essential

Essential storage makes the site work and remembers the privacy choices you make. It cannot be switched off in our preferences panel, because without it we could not remember that you said no. We use only two essential items, both in local storage. Neither is used to track you, and neither is shared with anyone.

Analytics

Analytics cookies count visits and show, in aggregate, which guides and sequences readers use, how they found us and where a page seems to lose people. That tells us what to write next and what to rewrite. Analytics is off until you opt in. If it is enabled, we will not use it to identify you, and we will not combine analytics data with your email address or account.

Advertising

Advertising cookies let ad partners, including Google, show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure whether ads are seen and clicked, detect ad fraud and, if you allow it, choose ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Advertising is off until you opt in, and it stays off if you have opted out of the sale or sharing of your personal information or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

If you do not allow advertising cookies, you may still see ads. They are chosen from the content of the page, not from your browsing history, and members see no ads at all.

Google's use of advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled and you allow advertising cookies, Google is one of the third-party vendors that serves ads on this site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at https://adssettings.google.com, and learn more about how Google uses information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can opt out of many other vendors' personalized advertising at https://optout.aboutads.info and https://optout.networkadvertising.org.

Those opt-outs apply to the browser you set them in and can be lost if you clear your cookies. They work alongside your choices in our own cookie preferences.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Sell or Share

If your browser or a browser extension sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we honor it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. Our site detects the signal and keeps advertising cookies off on that browser, even if you click Accept all, and the cookie preferences panel shows a note when this applies. GPC does not switch off analytics; you choose that separately.

Without GPC, you can opt out using the button in the Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information section of our Privacy Policy. That choice is stored as lsy_dnss_v1 and keeps advertising cookies off on that browser until you clear your browser storage.

Browser controls

You can also manage cookies directly in your browser. Most browsers let you:

  • see and delete cookies and site data, including our local storage entries;
  • block third-party cookies, which stops advertising cookies from other domains;
  • block all cookies and site data, which also stops us remembering your choices; and
  • browse in a private window that clears storage when you close it.

Look for the privacy or site data settings in Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge, or in the browser settings on your phone. If you delete our local storage entries, the consent banner will appear again on your next visit. Ad blockers and tracking protection may also block analytics and advertising scripts; the guides, sequences, timer and pacer all work without them.

Updates to this policy

We will update this policy, including the table, before we add a new analytics or advertising service or change how consent works. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version. If a change means we would like to use a category you have not agreed to, we will show the banner again and ask for your consent rather than assume it.

Questions about cookies or this policy can go to privacy@longshadowyoga.us or through the form on our Contact page. For how we handle personal information more broadly, read our Privacy Policy.
