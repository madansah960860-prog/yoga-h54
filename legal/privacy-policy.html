Who we are

This website, together with its pose library, sequences, guides, newsletter, membership, digital products and teacher directory, is operated by Long Shadow Yoga LLC, a small instructional publisher based at 418 Calle Sombra, Suite 3, Tucson, AZ 85705. In this policy, "we," "us" and "our" mean that company. We decide how and why the personal information described here is used, so we are responsible for it.

This policy covers information we collect through this website and through the emails we send. It does not cover other websites we link to, including retailers you reach through an affiliate link, the websites of teachers listed in our directory, or the advertisers whose ads appear here. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

The site is written for adults in the United States. We have tried to keep this policy readable from start to finish; if anything is unclear, the contact details are at the end.

What information we collect

We collect two kinds of information: what you choose to give us, and a limited amount that is collected automatically when your browser loads our pages. For each feature, we try to collect only what that feature needs.

Information you provide

  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address, the date you subscribed and the page you subscribed from. When you click the link in our confirmation email, we record that confirmation too.
  • Contact form and email: your name, email address, the topic you choose and whatever you write in your message. Please do not send us details about your health. We cannot give individual advice, and we do not need that information to answer you.
  • Membership and purchases: your name, email address, the password you create for your account, your plan (monthly or yearly), your billing country and postal code, your purchase history and the practice collections you save. Card details go to our payment processor, not to us (see Payments).
  • Teacher directory listings: the details you submit for your listing, such as your name, studio or business name, city and state, the styles you teach, the training you describe, your website or public contact details and a short bio. Most of this is published on the site by design, so please submit only what you are happy for the public to see. If you buy a Featured listing, we also keep billing records.
  • Partnership and sponsorship inquiries: your name, company, email address and the details of your proposal.

Information collected automatically

When your browser requests a page, some information is sent as a normal part of how the web works.

  • Device information: browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen size and language setting.
  • Log information: your IP address, the date and time of each request, the page requested, the page that referred you and whether the request succeeded. Our hosting provider records this in server logs, which we use for security and troubleshooting.
  • Cookie and local storage data, only as your consent allows: we store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so that we can respect it. Analytics cookies and advertising cookies are not set unless you opt in through our consent banner or cookie preferences. The Cookie Policy lists each one.

We do not collect precise geolocation. An IP address can suggest an approximate location, such as a city or region, and advertising partners may use it that way if you allow advertising cookies.

Information from other sources

Our payment processor tells us whether a payment succeeded and gives us limited details about it, described below. If you allow analytics or advertising cookies, our partners give us aggregated reports, such as how many people read a guide or how often an ad was shown. Those reports do not identify you to us.

How we use information

We use personal information for these purposes:

  • To provide what you asked for: sending the newsletter, answering your message, running your membership, delivering digital products and publishing your directory listing.
  • To manage payments, renewals and cancellations, and to send receipts and account notices.
  • To keep the site secure and working: spotting abuse, spam and fraud, fixing errors and keeping server logs.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which pages readers use, so we can decide what to write next and rewrite what is confusing. We use analytics cookies for this only if you opt in.
  • To show the advertising that pays for the free library. With your consent, ads may be personalized by our advertising partners. Without it, ads may still appear, but they are not chosen from your browsing history.
  • To comply with the law, such as keeping tax records, and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To enforce our Terms of Use and to protect the rights and safety of our readers, our team and others.

We do not use personal information to make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. We do not build profiles of individual readers ourselves, and we never use the content of your messages to target ads.

Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser; local storage is a similar browser feature. We use them in three categories:

  • Essential, always on: two local storage entries that remember your cookie choice (lsy_consent_v1) and, if you make one, your opt-out of sale or sharing (lsy_dnss_v1). Neither is used to track you or shared with anyone.
  • Analytics, off until you opt in: cookies that count visits and show which guides are used, in aggregate.
  • Advertising, off until you opt in: cookies that let ad partners, including Google, show and measure ads and, if you allow it, personalize them.

On your first visit, a banner offers three choices: Accept all, Reject non-essential and Manage preferences. No optional cookies or scripts load before you choose, and rejecting takes exactly one click, the same as accepting. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page or the button below. The Cookie Policy has the full list of names, purposes and lifespans.

Third-party vendors, including Google

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. They handle personal information on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of it to providing their service to us:

  • Website hosting and content delivery
  • Email delivery for the newsletter and account messages
  • Payment processing for memberships, digital products and Featured listings
  • Form handling and spam filtering
  • Analytics, only if you opt in
  • Advertising, only if you opt in

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order; when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to stop fraud or protect someone's safety; or as part of a business transfer, such as a merger or the sale of the site, in which case the new owner must honor this policy for the information it receives. We never rent, sell or trade our newsletter list.

Google's use of advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled and you allow advertising cookies, Google is one of the third-party vendors that serves ads on this site. Google requires us to tell you the following, and we agree you should know it:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can opt out of many other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance at https://optout.aboutads.info and the Network Advertising Initiative at https://optout.networkadvertising.org.

Those opt-outs are managed by the organizations that run them. They apply to the browser you use when you set them and may be lost if you clear your cookies. They work alongside, not instead of, the choices you make in our own cookie preferences.

Your choices and how to opt out

You can limit what is collected about you in several ways, and they can be combined:

  • Our consent tool: choose Reject non-essential on the banner, or switch analytics and advertising off in Cookie preferences. Your choice applies to the browser and device you are using.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser or a browser extension sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we honor it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information on that browser. Advertising cookies stay off, even if you click Accept all. You can still choose analytics separately.
  • Our Do Not Sell or Share control: see Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information below.
  • Browser controls: most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data, block third-party cookies or browse in a private window. Blocking third-party cookies does not affect our guides. Blocking all storage means we cannot remember your choices, so the banner will ask again.
  • Industry opt-out pages: the Google, Digital Advertising Alliance and Network Advertising Initiative pages listed above.
  • Email: every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Email and CAN-SPAM

We send two kinds of email. Marketing email, such as the newsletter, goes only to people who sign up and confirm their address. Transactional email, such as receipts, renewal reminders and account notices, goes to people who have bought something or hold an account, because it is part of that service.

Every marketing email we send:

  • identifies Long Shadow Yoga as the sender;
  • has a subject line that honestly reflects what is inside;
  • includes our physical mailing address, 418 Calle Sombra, Suite 3, Tucson, AZ 85705;
  • includes a working unsubscribe link that does not require you to log in; and
  • labels any sponsored section "Sponsored" at the top of that section.

We process unsubscribe requests promptly and always within 10 business days, as the CAN-SPAM Act requires. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop transactional messages about an active membership or a recent purchase.

Our email provider may record whether a message was opened and which links were clicked, using a tiny image and tracked links. We look at those figures in aggregate to see which topics readers find useful. You can limit open tracking by turning off automatic image loading in your email app. We never give or sell our subscriber list to sponsors or advertisers; a newsletter sponsor receives only aggregate figures, such as the number of subscribers and clicks.

Payments

Memberships, digital products and Featured listings are paid through a third-party payment processor. You enter your card or other payment details on the processor's secure form, and the processor handles them under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).

We do not receive or store full card numbers or card security codes. The processor shares with us only what we need to run your account: your name, email address, billing country and postal code, the type of card, its last four digits and expiry date, and the status of each charge or refund. The processor's own privacy policy governs how it handles your payment information.

Data security

We protect personal information with measures suited to a small publishing business. The whole site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to member accounts, email lists and payment dashboards is limited to the people who need it and protected with strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication. We choose service providers that make written security commitments, and we do not store payment card numbers at all.

No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires. Please use a unique password for your member account, and tell us right away if you think someone has used it without your permission.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our newsletter, membership, store and directory are meant for adults. Teenagers aged 13 to 17 may use the site only with the involvement of a parent or guardian, as our Terms of Use explain.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@longshadowyoga.us with "Child's information" in the subject line. Tell us what was submitted, for example the email address used for a newsletter sign-up, and we will delete it, normally within 10 business days. We will ask only for what we need to find the record. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

This section is our notice at collection and explains those rights. It applies in addition to the rest of this policy, and we extend these rights to California residents whether or not the law's size thresholds apply to us.

Sale and sharing

We do not sell personal information for money, and we never sell or share names, email addresses or payment details. However, if you allow advertising cookies, advertising partners such as Google may collect online identifiers, internet activity and approximate location through our site and use them to show you ads elsewhere. Under the CCPA, that can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, and it may also count as a "sale," because the site receives ad revenue in return. We therefore handle it as both, and you have the right to opt out at any time, as explained in the next section. We honor a Global Privacy Control signal as a valid opt-out. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes, as described in California Civil Code section 1798.83.

Your rights

  • Right to know and access: ask what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the purposes, the categories of recipients, and a copy of the specific pieces of information, in a portable format.
  • Right to delete: ask us to delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions, such as tax records we must keep.
  • Right to correct: ask us to correct personal information that is inaccurate.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: tell us not to sell or share your personal information. See Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose that gives rise to this right. The only sensitive information we hold is a member's log-in, which we use solely to let you sign in and keep your account secure. For that reason, we do not offer a separate "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not deny you service, charge you a different price or give you a different quality of service because you used any of these rights.

How to submit a request

  • Email privacy@longshadowyoga.us with "California privacy request" in the subject line.
  • Use the form on our Contact page and choose the privacy topic.
  • Write to us at Long Shadow Yoga LLC, Attn: Privacy, 418 Calle Sombra, Suite 3, Tucson, AZ 85705.

Tell us which right you want to use and give us the email address you use with us. We will confirm that we received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days. If we need more time, up to another 45 days, we will tell you why. You may ask us to tell you what we know about you twice in any 12-month period, free of charge.

How we verify requests

To protect you, we must confirm that a request to know, delete or correct information comes from the person the information is about. We will ask you to confirm that you control the email address linked to your newsletter subscription, membership or listing. For a request for specific pieces of information, we may also ask for one or two details that match our records, such as the date of a purchase. We use the information you give us for verification only to verify your request. You do not need an account to make a request, and a request to opt out of sale or sharing does not need to be verified.

Authorized agents

You may ask an authorized agent to make a request for you. We will ask the agent for proof that you gave signed permission, and we may ask you to verify your own identity directly with us or to confirm that you gave the agent permission. These steps do not apply if the agent holds a valid power of attorney under California Probate Code sections 4121 to 4130.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

You have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. On this site, the only activity that may count as a sale or sharing is advertising partners' use of cookies to show you personalized ads, so opting out turns that off.

Press the button below to opt out on this browser. We will save your choice in your browser's local storage, under the name lsy_dnss_v1, switch advertising cookies off and keep them off even if you later click Accept all. Your analytics choice stays as you set it. You may still see ads, but they will not be personalized using cookies.

Your opt-out applies to the browser and device you use when you press the button. If you use another browser or device, or clear your browser's site data, please opt out again there, or turn on Global Privacy Control so the choice travels with your browser.

Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control (GPC) is a setting offered by some browsers and browser extensions. It tells every site you visit that you do not want your personal information sold or shared. We honor GPC as a valid opt-out request. When our site detects the signal, advertising cookies stay off for that browser, the status message above says so, and our cookie preferences panel shows a note explaining why personalized advertising is unavailable. If GPC is on, you do not need to press the button.

Opt out by email

If you prefer, or if your browser does not run JavaScript, email privacy@longshadowyoga.us with "Do Not Sell or Share" in the subject line. We will apply the opt-out to any personal information linked to your email address, such as your newsletter subscription or membership, and confirm when it is done. We act on opt-out requests within 15 business days.

Privacy rights in other US states

A growing number of states give residents privacy rights similar to California's, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Iowa, Delaware, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Nebraska, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky and Rhode Island, with more laws taking effect each year. Depending on where you live, these rights can include the right to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data and get access to it;
  • correct inaccuracies in your personal data;
  • delete your personal data;
  • get a copy of your personal data in a portable format; and
  • opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not carry out profiling of that kind.

We extend these rights to residents of every US state, whether or not a particular state law applies to us. Use the same request methods described in the California section above. We honor Global Privacy Control as a universal opt-out signal for targeted advertising and sales, as Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon and several other states require. We do not sell covered information as defined by Nevada law, but Nevada residents may still send us an opt-out request, and we will record it.

If we decline a request, we will explain why. You may appeal by replying to our decision with the word "Appeal" within 60 days. We will respond to your appeal within the time your state's law sets, and no later than 60 days. If you are not satisfied with the result, you may contact your state's Attorney General.

Visitors from the EU, EEA and UK

This site is directed at readers in the United States, and we do not offer goods or services specifically to people in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. If you visit from those regions, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the UK GDPR may still apply to some of our processing, so we want you to know how it works.

Legal bases

  • Consent: analytics and advertising cookies, and the newsletter. You can withdraw consent at any time; doing so does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Contract: running your membership, delivering digital products and publishing a Featured listing you paid for.
  • Legitimate interests: answering messages, keeping the site secure and free of abuse, and keeping server logs. We weigh these interests against your rights, and you can object.
  • Legal obligation: keeping tax and accounting records.

Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data; to restrict or object to its processing; to data portability; to withdraw consent at any time; and to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority where you live or work.

International transfers

We and our service providers are based in, and store data in, the United States. When personal data is transferred to the United States from the EU, the EEA or the UK, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the recipient is certified, or on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, together with the UK addendum, where it is not.

How to contact us from the EU, EEA or UK

Send requests to privacy@longshadowyoga.us. We will respond within one month, which may be extended by two further months for complex requests; if so, we will tell you why. Because the site is directed at the United States, we have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change, for example when we add a new vendor or a feature that uses personal information. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, such as using information for a new purpose, we will post a notice on the site before it takes effect and, if you are a subscriber or member, email you. We will ask for your consent where the law requires it. We keep earlier versions of this policy and will send you one on request.

Contact us about your data

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy can reach us in any of these ways:

  • Email: privacy@longshadowyoga.us
  • Mail: Long Shadow Yoga LLC, Attn: Privacy, 418 Calle Sombra, Suite 3, Tucson, AZ 85705
