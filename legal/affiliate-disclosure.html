Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated 11/16/2026

Who pays for this site, how we label it wherever it appears, and the rules we keep so that money never quietly shapes what we teach.

The short version

This site is free to read because it earns money in several ways. Some of them involve brands and businesses, so the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) expects us to tell you about those relationships clearly and in a place you will notice. We would want to anyway. Here is the short version:

  • We show display ads, and every ad space is labeled "Advertisement."
  • Some links to props are affiliate links, and each one is labeled right where it appears.
  • Sponsored content is labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page.
  • Featured teacher listings are paid, and they say so.
  • No one can pay to change our safety guidance or to be ranked above anyone else.

How this site is funded

Here is every way the site earns money, in plain terms.

  • Display advertising. The free pose library, all sequences, the sequence timer and the breath pacer are paid for mainly by ads. We say so openly, label every ad and keep ads away from instructions and controls.
  • Optional paid membership. Shade Membership costs $7 per month or $60 per year, in US dollars. Members get ad-free browsing, downloadable sequence PDFs, saved practice collections, printable pose cards and 20% off digital products. You can cancel at any time. See Pricing & Membership.
  • Affiliate commissions. When we link to mats, blocks, straps or bolsters, we may earn a commission if you buy. We disclose this at the point of every link, not only on this page.
  • Paid digital products. We sell the First 30 Days beginner program ($29), the Chair & Wall Sequence Bundle ($14) and the Evening Practice Bundle ($14). These are our own products, and we say so whenever we mention them.
  • Sponsored content and brand partnerships. A business may pay us to feature something. Anything sponsored is labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the page or newsletter section.
  • Teacher directory. Listings are free. Teachers can pay $19 per month for a Featured listing, which is always marked "Featured listing (paid placement)."

None of these income sources decides what we teach, how we describe a pose or which versions we offer. Everything you need to practice stays free.

Affiliate links

An affiliate link is a link to a retailer's product page that carries a code identifying us. If you click it and buy something, the retailer may pay us a small commission on that sale. The price you pay is exactly what you would pay if you went to the retailer directly. It costs you nothing extra, and you never have to use our links; searching for the same item yourself works just as well.

We use affiliate links only for practice props: mats, blocks, straps, bolsters, blankets and similar items. We do not use them for supplements, or for any product that makes health or appearance claims.

Our affiliate links usually point to search results or category pages at online retailers rather than to a single brand, so you can compare options and buy whatever fits your budget and your body. We may belong to affiliate programs run by retailers or affiliate networks. When a program requires specific wording, we add it to this page.

The labels you will see

Every affiliate link on the site has this sentence directly next to it, not tucked into a footer or hidden behind a click:

Affiliate link: we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you.

Affiliate links are also marked in the page code as sponsored, which tells search engines that the link is commercial. Elsewhere on the site, you will see these labels:

  • "Advertisement" above every ad space.
  • "Sponsored" in a dark banner at the top of any sponsored page, and at the top of any sponsored newsletter section, with the sponsor's name.
  • "Featured listing (paid placement)" on every paid teacher listing.

If you ever find a paid relationship on the site that is not labeled, it is a mistake, and we want to know. Please tell us using the details at the end of this page.

How we choose products

Editorial decisions come first and commissions come second. In practice, that means:

  • We decide which props to mention based on what a pose or sequence needs, before we check whether an affiliate link exists.
  • We describe generic product types and what to look for, such as size, material, grip and weight, rather than pushing a particular brand.
  • We do not accept payment for rankings, for placement in our guides or for favorable descriptions. You will not find a ranked list of mats here, paid or unpaid.
  • Every pose works with household items too: a folded blanket, a firm cushion, a belt or a sturdy chair without wheels. Buying anything is optional.
  • Retailers and brands never see or approve our content before it is published.
  • If a product we link to changes, or readers tell us it has problems, we review the link and remove it if it no longer fits.

We do not accept free products in exchange for coverage. If we ever describe a product we were given, we will say so at the start of the description.

Advertising and ad labeling

Display ads pay for the free library. They are supplied by third-party ad networks, such as Google, once our accounts are approved. Our rules for ads are:

  • Every ad space is labeled "Advertisement" and styled to look different from our own content.
  • Ads never appear inside pose instructions, beside the sequence timer or breath pacer controls, or next to buttons, forms or navigation.
  • We show no ads on our legal pages, contact page, newsletter page or pricing page.
  • We reserve the space for each ad in advance, so the page does not jump while you read.
  • Where our ad partners let us, we block ad categories that conflict with our approach, such as dieting and supplement ads.
  • We do not choose individual ads, and an ad appearing here is not an endorsement.
  • Ads that use cookies to personalize what you see load only if you consent. Our Cookie Policy explains how.
  • Members browse without ads.

If you see an ad that seems misleading or makes health claims, please send us a screenshot or the address the ad leads to, so we can report and block it.

Sponsored content and brand partnerships

A brand partnership means a business pays us to create or feature something, such as a sponsored article, a sponsored newsletter section or a sponsored sequence. Our rules are:

  • Sponsored pages carry a "Sponsored" label at the top of the page, before the content starts, along with the sponsor's name. In the newsletter, the label sits at the top of the sponsored section.
  • We write sponsored content ourselves, in our usual voice, and it meets the same standards as everything else on the site.
  • Sponsors cannot review, edit or remove our safety guidance, contraindications or disclaimers, and sponsored content never makes health claims.
  • We reject sponsors from medical, weight-loss, supplement and detox businesses, and from any business whose products conflict with our approach.
  • We do not publish fake sponsored posts, undisclosed endorsements or paid reviews.
  • Sponsorship and newsletter sponsorship rates are available on request from partners@longshadowyoga.us. More detail is on our Work With Us page.

If a sponsor asks for something that breaks these rules, we decline the partnership.

Featured teacher listings

Our teacher directory is free to join. Teachers can pay $19 per month for a Featured listing, which is shown first among listings in their state and is always marked "Featured listing (paid placement)." Featured placement buys position and that label only. It is not an endorsement, a review or a recommendation, and we do not verify any teacher's training or credentials, whether their listing is free or paid. Our Terms of Use say more about how the directory works.

Questions or concerns

  • General questions: hello@longshadowyoga.us
  • Partnerships and sponsorships: partners@longshadowyoga.us
  • Online: the form on our Contact page
