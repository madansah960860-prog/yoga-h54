Our team are yoga teachers and editors, not doctors or physical therapists. If you have pain, an injury, a health condition or a question about pregnancy, please talk with a qualified healthcare professional before you practice.

What to expect after you write

Messages are read in the order they arrive on business days. Anything that affects safety guidance on a published page goes to the front of the line.

TopicWhat to expect
General questionA reply within two business days.
Correction or feedbackA reply within two business days. Safety-related corrections are reviewed first.
